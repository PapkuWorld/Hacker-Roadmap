# Human-Level AI

**Category:** Capability Levels

Human-level AI is AI that matches human performance across the full range of intellectual tasks, not just one narrow benchmark. Today AI beats humans at many specific tasks, but not yet across the board. This page tracks the milestones, benchmarks, forecasts and labs.
