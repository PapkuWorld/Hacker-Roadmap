# MMD (MikuMikuDance)

[← Back to Roadmap](../../README.md) · [Creative](README.md) · [Animation & 3D](animation-3d.md)

MikuMikuDance (MMD) is a free 3D animation program for making dance and music videos with Vocaloid and custom models.
